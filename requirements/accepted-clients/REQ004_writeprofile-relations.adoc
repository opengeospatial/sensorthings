.WriteProfile relations
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/accepted-clients/writeprofile-relations
part:: An implementation SHALL allow each WriteProfile to have the direct associations listed in <<tab-writeprofile-relations>>, and SHALL allow each Client, ObservedProperty, Datastream and MultiDatastream to have the direct association to WriteProfile listed in <<tab-relations-to-writeprofile>>.
part:: Each WriteProfile SHALL be associated with at least one Client.
