====
[%metadata]

part:: add:[If the implementation claims the Party Context conformance class, the privacy notice (<<req_gdpr_privacy-notice>>) SHALL state that the implementation stores PartyLocation and PartyActivity entities, the legal basis for storing them (item (c) of <<req_gdpr_privacy-notice>>), that they are withheld from others unless the data subject authorizes their release by an Agreement, that `retentionTime` and the deletion of the Party remove them, and that values released to recipients cannot be recalled by withdrawing the Agreement.]
