[[req_dggs_cell-release]]
[requirement]
.Release of Cell associations
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/dggs/cell-release
part:: For a Cell association of an attributable entity linked to the zone `c` at level `L`, the implementation SHALL determine the release level for a requester `r` that is not the data subject as follows: if an Agreement of the Party exists that is effective for the property path, the operation `publish` and `r`, the release level is the largest `zoneLevel` of add:[the ReleaseProfiles of ]those Agreements, where an absent `zoneLevel` counts as `L`; otherwise, the release level is the public zone level. The release level SHALL NOT be larger than `L`, and SHALL be reduced according to <<req_dggs_privacy-zones>>.
part:: The implementation SHALL release the association as the ancestor of `c` at the release level (or `c` itself if the release level equals `L`). Where the ancestor is coarser than `coarsestZoneLevel`, the association SHALL NOT be released.
part:: This requirement SHALL apply to every response, including `$expand`, `$select`, navigation from Observation, Datastream and MultiDatastream to Cell, and MQTT payloads.
part:: The data subject SHALL receive the stored association according to <<req_gdpr_access-right>>.
====
