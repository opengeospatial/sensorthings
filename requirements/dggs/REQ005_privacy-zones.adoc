[requirement]
.Privacy zones
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/dggs/privacy-zones
part:: The privacy zones of a Party are the zone identifiers in `privacyZones` of all del:[Agreements of the Party whose `status` is `active` and whose `expiryTime` is absent or later than the current time, irrespective of their `effectiveTime`.] add:[ReleaseProfiles of the Party, whether or not an Agreement is associated with them. A privacy zone SHALL take effect when its ReleaseProfile is created, and SHALL cease to take effect when its ReleaseProfile is deleted.]
part:: If the stored Cell `c` of a Cell association, or the Cell that contains a stored position of an indirect location property, is equal to or a descendant of a privacy zone `z` of the Party, the release level for a requester that is not the data subject SHALL NOT be larger than the zone level of `z`.
part:: add:[If the implementation claims the Party Context conformance class, a PartyLocation of the Party whose `position` lies inside a privacy zone SHALL be withheld from every requester that is not the data subject (<<req_party-context_release>>).]
====
