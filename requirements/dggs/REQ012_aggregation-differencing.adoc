[requirement]
.Protection against differencing
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/dggs/aggregation-differencing
part:: If the implementation claims the conformance class `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-dggs/1.0/conf/aggregation`, for each released aggregate Observation of a Cell `c` and an interval `i`, the implementation SHALL ensure that among the parts of that aggregate the number of withheld parts is not exactly one. The parts are (a) the aggregates of the children of `c` for `i`, together with the aggregate of the raw Observations linked directly to `c` for `i`; and (b) the aggregates of `c` for each configured shorter interval that partitions `i`.
part:: Where this condition is not met, the implementation SHALL withhold a further part or the aggregate of `c` for `i`.
====
