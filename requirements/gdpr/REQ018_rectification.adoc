[[req_gdpr_rectification]]
[requirement]
.Right to rectification
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/gdpr/rectification
part:: The data subject of a Party `P` SHALL be able to update, by HTTP PATCH, the direct personal data properties of `P` and of the party-specific License instances attributable to `P`, subject to <<req_gdpr_store-direct>>.
part:: The implementation SHALL reject such an update by a requester that is not the data subject with HTTP 403 and SHALL leave the entity unchanged.
====
