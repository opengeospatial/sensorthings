.Personal data register
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/gdpr/personal-data-register
part:: The implementation SHALL treat the property values listed in <<tab-register>> as personal data whenever they belong to an entity that is attributable to a Party whose `role` is `individual`.
part:: An entity SHALL be considered attributable to a Party `P` if it is one of the following: (1) `P` itself; (2) an Agreement add:[or a ReleaseProfile ]associated with `P`; (3) a Datastream or MultiDatastream associated with `P`, or an Observation of such a Datastream or MultiDatastream; (4) a FeatureOfInterest associated with at least one Observation listed under (3); (5) a Thing associated with `P`, or a Thing that is not associated with any Party and that is associated with a Datastream or MultiDatastream listed under (3), including the Locations and HistoricalLocations of such a Thing; (6) an ObservationGroup or Campaign associated with `P`; (7) a party-specific License instance (a License that is not a template) associated with an entity listed under (3) or (6); (8) a Relation whose Subject or Object is an Observation listed under (3). add:[If the implementation claims the Party Context conformance class: (9) a PartyLocation or PartyActivity associated with `P`.]
part:: An entity that is attributable to more than one Party whose `role` is `individual` SHALL be subject to the requirements of this Requirements Class for each of those Parties. A property value of such an entity SHALL be released to a requester only if the release conditions are met for every one of those Parties.
part:: The requirements of this Requirements Class SHALL NOT apply to entities that are attributable only to Parties whose `role` is `institutional` or `system`.
part:: add:[If the implementation implements the Party Context conformance class of <<OGC26-040>> and accepts PartyLocation or PartyActivity entities associated with a Party whose `role` is `individual`, it SHALL also implement the Party Context conformance class of this Profile.]
====
