.Data protection impact assessment
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/rec/gdpr/dpia
part:: The operator SHOULD carry out a data protection impact assessment before deploying the implementation, because the processing of location and time data of citizen observers can amount to systematic monitoring.
