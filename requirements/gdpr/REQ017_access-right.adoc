[[req_gdpr_access-right]]
[requirement]
.Right of access
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/gdpr/access-right
part:: The implementation SHALL release to the data subject of a Party `P` all stored values of all entities attributable to `P`, without omission, generalization or withholding, except where <<req_gdpr_personal-data-register>> requires otherwise for an entity that is also attributable to another Party.
part:: The implementation SHALL release to the data subject the Agreements of `P` when the data subject requests `Parties?$filter=authId eq me()&$expand=Agreements`.
====
