[requirement]
.Privacy notice
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/gdpr/privacy-notice
part:: The privacy notice SHALL be an HTML page in English that is retrievable without authentication.
part:: The privacy notice SHALL state at least: (a) the identity and contact details of the controller and, where applicable, of the data protection officer; (b) the personal data register, consisting of the properties of <<tab-register>> and any additional properties that the operator classifies as personal data, such as `Observation/result`, `Observation/parameters` or `Sensor/metadata`; (c) for each purpose of processing, its legal basis, including the legal basis for publishing `Party/@iot.id`, `Party/role` and the Party associations, for storing indirect location and time properties and free-text values, and for releasing generalized values; (d) the documents referenced by `Agreement/definition` offered by the implementation; (e) the recipients, including that released data are public where `audience` is `public`; del:[(f) the retention periods, including the retention of records of withdrawn Agreements;] add:[(f) the retention periods, including the advertised `retentionPeriod` and `inactivityPeriod` (<<req_gdpr_retention>>) and the retention of records of withdrawn Agreements;] (g) the location and time generalization methods and resolutionsadd:[, and how the data subject controls the release with ReleaseProfiles, including privacy areas]; (h) per entity type, whether attributable entities are deleted or anonymized on deletion of a Party; (i) the rights of the data subject (access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, withdrawal of consent at any time, lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority) and how each is exercised with this API; (j) how special categories of personal data and personal data of children are handled; (k) the authentication sources that the implementation accepts and, for each Party whose `role` is `institutional` or `system`, the organisation or system it represents; (l) for each Campaign whose Party has the `role` `institutional`, whether that organisation acts as separate controller, joint controller or processor for the data of the participants, with a link to its privacy policy; (m) the values of `Campaign/classification` that the implementation treats as sensitive.
====
