.Effective Agreement
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/gdpr/agreement-effective
part:: The implementation SHALL consider an Agreement effective for a property path `p`, an operation `o` and a requester `r` if and only if all of the following conditions hold: `status` is `active`; `effectiveTime` is not later than the current time; `expiryTime` is absent or later than the current time; `coveredProperties` contains `p`; `permittedOperations` contains `o`; and, if `o` is `publish`, either `audience` is `public`, or `audience` is `authenticated` and `r` is authenticated. If the implementation claims the Accepted Clients conformance class, the additional condition of <<req_accepted-clients_client-restricted-agreements>> SHALL also hold.
part:: An Agreement that is associated with a Campaign `c` SHALL be effective only for property values of `c` itself and of the entities that belong to `c`: the Datastreams, MultiDatastreams and ObservationGroups associated with `c`, their Observations, the FeaturesOfInterest of those Observations, the Things of those Datastreams and MultiDatastreams with their Locations and HistoricalLocations, the License instances associated with any of them, and the Relations whose Subject or Object is one of those Observations. An Agreement that is not associated with a Campaign SHALL be effective independent of Campaigns.
part:: The implementation SHALL NOT use any other information, including Campaign `termsOfUse` or `privacyPolicy`, ObservationGroup `privacyPolicy` or License entities, as a substitute for an effective Agreement where a requirement of this Requirements Class demands one.
====
