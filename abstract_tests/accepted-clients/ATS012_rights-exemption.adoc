.Exemption for data subject rights
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/conf/accepted-clients/rights-exemption
target:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/accepted-clients/rights-exemption
test-purpose:: Verify that the client catalog cannot block the exercise of data subject rights.
test-method-type:: Functional

[.component,class=test method]
=====
[.component,class=step]
--
Obtain a credential for U1 that resolves to a client identifier that is not in the catalog.
--

[.component,class=step]
--
With that credential, create an Agreement for P1 and withdraw it. Verify success status codes.
--

[.component,class=step]
--
With that credential, PATCH `Parties(P1)` with a new `displayName` (with an Agreement permitting `store`). Verify a success status code.
--

[.component,class=step]
--
With that credential, DELETE an Observation of D1. Verify a success status code.
--

[.component,class=step]
--
With that credential, create an Observation on D1. Verify HTTP 403.
--
