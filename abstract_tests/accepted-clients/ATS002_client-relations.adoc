.Client relations
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/conf/accepted-clients/client-relations
target:: http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus-gdpr/1.0/req/accepted-clients/client-relations
test-purpose:: Verify the navigation between Client, Party and WriteProfile.
test-method-type:: Functional

[.component,class=test method]
=====
[.component,class=step]
--
As A, request `Clients(C1)?$expand=Party,WriteProfiles`. Verify that the provider Party is expanded, that its `role` is `institutional`, and that WP1 is contained in `WriteProfiles`.
--

[.component,class=step]
--
As A, request `Parties(PP)/Clients`, where PP is the provider Party, and verify that C1 is contained.
--

[.component,class=step]
--
As OP, POST a Client that is associated with a Party whose `role` is `individual`. Verify HTTP 400.
--
