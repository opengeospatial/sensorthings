del:[Overview]

del:[This annex maps the technical measures of the Conformance Class *GDPR* Conformance Class to <<GDPR>>. It also lists controller obligations that this Standard does not specify and cannot test.]

del:[Conformance to this Standard is not a certification of compliance with <<GDPR>>. Legal compliance of a deployment remains with the controller.]

del:[This Standard does not define a consent entity. <<OGC22-022r2>> places identity data outside `Party` and advertises a GDPR compliance note as part of the Business Logic. The legal-basis gate of `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/gdpr/legal-basis` may be implemented by the authentication system that supplies `Party/authId`. A `License` entity is a reuse condition for data that is already readable. It is not a legal basis for processing `Location` of a `Thing` owned by an individual party, or for processing `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`.]

del:[Technical measures specified by this Standard]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: <<GDPR>> provision — Obligation — STAplus 1.1 measure]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 5(1)(b) — Purpose limitation — Landing-page privacy notice and legal-basis gate. This Standard does not store collection purpose on `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`. `Share/purpose` records the purpose of a disclosure.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 5(1)(c) — Data minimisation — `Share` targets exactly one entity. Location and activity are separate. An `Observation` of an individual party has a `PartyLocation`. `Thing/Location` of an individual party's `Thing` is owner-only. `FeatureOfInterest` is public. If `FeatureOfInterest` geometry would be the observing user's location, it is null and the position is stored in `PartyLocation`.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 5(1)(e) — Storage limitation — Optional `retentionTime` on `PartyLocation` and `PartyActivity`, with delete when that time is reached. `HistoricalLocation/time` plus landing-page `retentionPeriod` for an individual party's `Thing`, with delete when that instant is reached. A current `Location` is not auto-deleted. Owner bulk delete of `Thing` locations and of party context.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 5(1)(f), Art. 32 — Integrity and confidentiality — Authentication. Owner-only CRUD. No leak through `$expand`, navigation, deep insert, or MQTT. Immutable `Party/authId` as specified in <<OGC22-022r2>>.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 5(2) — Accountability — Published Business Logic, including the GDPR compliance note of <<OGC22-022r2>>. Landing-page privacy notice and controller identification. This annex.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 6, Art. 7 — Legal basis; conditions for consent — Create of an individual `Party`, of that party's `Thing` locations, and of `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity` is refused unless a current legal basis exists for that `Party`. The mechanism may be the identity system. Consent is not an STA entity.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 12–14 — Transparent information — Landing-page `privacyPolicy` href, controller name and contact, `retentionPeriod`, and accepted `legalBasis` codes. `Campaign/privacyPolicy` remains available as specified in <<OGC22-022r2>>.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 15, Art. 20 — Access; data portability — Authenticated `$expand` export of the party's things, locations, activities, shares, and linked observation identifiers.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 16 — Rectification — Update of own `Thing` locations and of own `PartyLocation` and `PartyActivity`. `Party/authId` remains immutable.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 17 — Erasure — Delete of own entities, cascade of shares and observation links, bulk delete of a thing's locations and of a party's context, and MQTT retained-message clearance.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 18, Art. 21 — Restriction; objection — `restricted` on `PartyLocation` and `PartyActivity` suspends `Share` access. Delete of a `Share` or reaching `Share/endTime` revokes a disclosure.]

del:[Technical measures of the GDPR Conformance Class mapped to <<GDPR>>: Art. 25 — Data protection by design and by default — GDPR Conformance Class is required when `Party/role` is `individual`. Personal context is owner-only unless a valid `Share` grants access.]
