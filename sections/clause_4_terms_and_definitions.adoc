== Terms, definitions and abbreviated terms

This document uses the terms defined in OGC Policy Directive 49, which is based on the ISO/IEC Directives, Part 2, Rules for the structure and drafting of International Standards. In particular, the word "`shall`" (not "`must`") is the verb form used to indicate a requirement to be strictly followed to conform to this document and OGC documents do not use the equivalent phrases in the ISO/IEC Directives, Part 2.

This document also uses terms defined in the OGC Standard for Modular specifications (OGC 08-131r3), also known as the 'ModSpec'. The definitions of terms such as standard, specification, requirement, and conformance test are provided in the ModSpec.

This document uses the terms Campaign, License, ObservationGroup, Ownership, Party and Relation as defined in <<OGC22-022r2>>.

For the purposes of this document, the following additional terms and definitions apply.

=== Terms and definitions

==== personal data

any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ('data subject'); an identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural or social identity of that natural person

[.source]
<<GDPR>>

NOTE: This definition is GDPR Article 4(1).

==== data subject

natural person to whom personal data relate

NOTE: In this document, the data subject of a Party whose `role` is `individual` is the natural person represented by that Party. A requester is the data subject of a Party if it is authenticated and its user identifier (`REMOTE_USER`) is identical to the `authId` of that Party.

==== processing

any operation or set of operations which is performed on personal data or on sets of personal data, whether or not by automated means, such as collection, recording, organisation, structuring, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment or combination, restriction, erasure or destruction

[.source]
<<GDPR>>

NOTE: This definition is GDPR Article 4(2).

==== consent

any freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous indication of the data subject's wishes by which he or she, by a statement or by a clear affirmative action, signifies agreement to the processing of personal data relating to him or her

[.source]
<<GDPR>>

NOTE: This definition is GDPR Article 4(11).

==== controller

natural or legal person, public authority, agency or other body which, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data

[.source]
<<GDPR>>, modified — In this document, the controller is the operator of the STAplus endpoint.

==== direct personal data

personal data contained in a property value that, on its own, names, identifies or describes the data subject

EXAMPLE: `Party/displayName`, `License/attributionText` of a party-specific License instance.

==== indirect personal data

personal data contained in a location or time property value that does not identify the data subject on its own but relates to the data subject through the attribution of the entity and allows the data subject to be identified or profiled when combined with other information

==== attributable entity

entity that is associated with a Party through the ownership model of STAplus according to the rules of <<req_gdpr_personal-data-register>>

==== Agreement

entity that records the authorization, given by a data subject, for the storage or the publication of specified personal data for a specified purpose

==== effective Agreement

Agreement that fulfils all conditions of <<req_gdpr_agreement-effective>> for a given property path, operation and requester

==== release

inclusion of a property value in an HTTP response body or in an MQTT message payload

==== withheld entity

entity that the implementation does not release to a requester: it is omitted from collections, `$expand` results and MQTT messages, a request to its URL is answered with HTTP 404, and it is not counted in `@iot.count`

==== generalization

deterministic transformation of a location or time value that reduces its precision to an advertised resolution

==== principal

authenticated user or system whose permanent and unique identifier, taken from the authentication, becomes `Party/authId`

NOTE: A principal is a natural person for a Party whose `role` is `individual`, and an organisation or an autonomously acting system for a Party whose `role` is `institutional` or `system`.

==== operator-managed identity

identity of a principal that the operator of the endpoint has created and controls, for example a local account, as opposed to an identity that a person registers with an external identity provider

==== requester

user or system on whose behalf the implementation processes an HTTP request or delivers an MQTT message; a requester is either the data subject, another authenticated user, or unauthenticated

==== template License

License entity created by the implementation, which is read-only

[.source]
<<OGC22-022r2>>

==== party-specific License instance

License entity constructed by a Party from a template License, for a License whose `definition` identifies an attribution license, with a non-empty `attributionText`

[.source]
<<OGC22-022r2>>

==== client

application that requests access to the implementation on behalf of a user, identified by a client identifier that is bound to the credential of the request, for example an OAuth 2.0 client identifier

[.source]
<<RFC6749>>, modified — reduced to the aspects relevant for this document.

==== accepted client

client release for which the client catalog of an endpoint contains a Client entity with `status` `accepted`

==== client catalog

set of Client and WriteProfile entities maintained by one endpoint

==== catalog administrator

requester authorized by the operator of an endpoint to create, update and delete Client and WriteProfile entities

==== write profile

declared and enforced write behaviour of one or more clients, consisting of the permitted entity types, ObservedProperties and observation types and of JSON Schemas for the written entities

==== provenance

association of a Datastream or MultiDatastream with the write profile under which it was created

==== Cell

SensorThings entity that represents one zone of the Discrete Global Grid System deployed by the implementation

[.source]
<<OGC26-057>>

==== zone level

hierarchical resolution of a zone in the deployed grid system; a smaller zone level is a coarser zone

[.source]
<<OGC26-057>>

==== public zone level

zone level at which Cell associations and generalized geometries are released to requesters other than the data subject when no Agreement authorizes a different zone level

==== release level

zone level at which a Cell association or a generalized geometry is released to a given requester

==== privacy zone

zone chosen by a data subject inside which no location of the data subject is released at a level finer than the zone itself

==== contributor

Party whose raw Observations contribute to an aggregate Observation of a Cell

==== property path

string of the form `EntityType/property` that identifies a property of an entity type, for example `Party/displayName`
