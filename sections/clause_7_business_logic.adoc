== STAplus Business Logic Requirements

=== Overview

The STAplus extension is defined based on requirements from the Citizen Science community. One major requirement from the Citizen Science community is that an implementation of STAplus can be used by many users simultaneously to read, create, update, and delete observations. To ensure integrity of all entities and to prevent inconsistencies in the observations, it is important that an implementation is not only compliant with this Standard but also has functionality that ensures entity consistency.

Even though the business logic can be very complex and most likely depend on many factors, it should be possible add:[to] del:[providing] add:[provide] a hint to developers and add:[to] del:[guiding] add:[guide] end users of the implementation in case a request results in an unexpected response.

As a machineadd:[-]readable and understandable description of the implemented business logic is preferred, the idea of the business logic requirements class is to allow the description of the logic in English text.

NOTE: add:[The STAplus 1.0 Corrigendum Standard does not determine which STAplus or SensorThings entities or properties are personal data. Values such as location and time can be personal data depending on the deployment. The operator of the endpoint is responsible for identifying personal data and for their lawful processing, by access control, by a legal basis, or by both. That applies to HTTP operations and to MQTT publish and subscribe. A `Party` entity is deleted only when the authenticated user is associated with that `Party/authId`. A `$filter` that compares `authId` with a literal value, including `$filter=authId eq ''`, is rejected with HTTP 400. Advertising this personal-data note as part of the Business Logic exposes the operator's statement on the endpoint. Claiming the Business Logic Conformance Class does not constitute GDPR compliance of the deployment.]

NOTE: add:[`License` entities created by the implementation are templates and are read-only. When `License/definition` does not identify an attribution license, the template is associated as-is; a party-specific instance and `attributionText` are not used. When `License/definition` identifies an attribution license, each use is a party-specific instance of the template with a non-empty `attributionText`; the writable properties of that instance are `attributionText` and `description`, which the associated `Party` may update. Creator identification is always that `attributionText`. `Party/displayName` and `Party/authId` are not used as creator identification. For an aggregate `MultiDatastream` whose license requires attribution, contributors whose source license requires attribution are identified by reference to those source `License` instances, not by copying their `attributionText`. The STAplus 1.0 Corrigendum Standard does not determine whether `attributionText` is personal data or the legal basis for publishing it. Lawful processing of that text is the operator's responsibility, as specified in <<req-business-logic-gdpr-note>>. Advertising this License attribution note as part of the Business Logic exposes the capture rule on the endpoint.]

add:[The advertised Business Logic SHALL state all of the following:]

* add:[The STAplus 1.0 Corrigendum Standard does not determine which STAplus or SensorThings entities or properties are personal data.]
* add:[The operator of the endpoint is responsible for identifying personal data and for their lawful processing.]
* add:[Lawful processing is achieved by access control, by a legal basis determined by the operator, or by both.]
* add:[Access control and lawful processing apply to HTTP operations and to MQTT publish and subscribe.]

[[req-business-logic-attribution]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: {identifier-corrigendum}/req/business-logic/attribution

add:[The advertised Business Logic SHALL state all of the following:]

* add:[`License` entities created by the implementation are templates. A template is read-only.]
* add:[When `License/definition` does not identify an attribution license, each use of that `License` on a `Datastream`, `MultiDatastream`, `ObservationGroup`, or `Campaign` SHALL associate the template. The implementation SHALL NOT require a party-specific `License` instance or a non-empty `attributionText`.]
* add:[When `License/definition` identifies an attribution license, a `Party` SHALL construct its own `License` instance from the template. Each use of that license on a `Datastream`, `MultiDatastream`, `ObservationGroup`, or `Campaign` SHALL be such an instance. That instance SHALL have a non-empty `attributionText`.]
* add:[For a party-specific instance of an attribution license, the writable properties are `attributionText` and `description`. Those properties MAY be updated only by the `Party` that is associated with the `License` through a `Datastream`, `MultiDatastream`, `Thing`, `ObservationGroup`, or `Campaign`.]
* add:[When `License/definition` identifies an attribution license, creator identification SHALL always be the `attributionText` of that `License` instance. `Party/displayName` and `Party/authId` SHALL NOT be used as creator identification.]
* add:[A template `License` SHALL NOT store a party-specific name in `attributionText`.]
* add:[The `Party` of a derived or aggregate `Datastream` or `MultiDatastream` is the producer of that derived work. When the `License/definition` of an aggregate `MultiDatastream` identifies an attribution license, that `License` SHALL identify contributors whose source `License/definition` identifies an attribution license by reference to those source `License` instances, not by copying their `attributionText`. Those references SHALL be obtained by expanding each such source `Datastream` or `MultiDatastream` to its `License`, or each grouped `Observation` to its `Datastream` and `License`.]
* add:[The STAplus 1.0 Corrigendum Standard does not determine whether `attributionText` used as creator citation is personal data, or the legal basis for publishing it. Lawful processing of that text is the operator's responsibility, as specified in <<req-business-logic-gdpr-note>>.]
====
