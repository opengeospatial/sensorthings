del:[STAplus Share Requirements]

del:[Overview]

del:[The GDPR Requirements Class restricts Read, Create, Update, and Delete of `PartyLocation` and `PartyActivity` to the linked party. A contributor may still need to grant named other parties access to that personal context, for example a campaign coordinator or a research group. A `License` expresses reuse of data that is already readable. It does not grant Read of a GDPR-locked entity.]

del:[The `Share` entity is a grant. The grantor is the `Party` that owns the target. The `Recipients` are the parties that receive the permission. A `Share` targets exactly one of `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`. To share both a location and an activity, the grantor creates two `Share` entities. To share several location-activity pairs, the grantor creates one `Share` per entity.]

del:[This clause defines the `Share` entity and the access that a valid `Share` grants. That access is an exception to the GDPR Requirements Class. It is not a Business Logic extension of GDPR.]

del:[NOTE: The Requirements Classes in this section are part of the Conformance Class Conformance Class *Sharing Party Context*.]

del:[Requirements Class *Share*]

del:[The `Share` entity records a permission that a party grants to one or more other parties on exactly one `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`. The `permission` property is `read` or `update`. The value `update` includes `read`. The `purpose` property records why the grant exists. The optional `endTime` expires the grant. Deleting the `Share` revokes it.]

del:[Requirements Class `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req-class/share` (Share)]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/share/properties`: Each `Share` entity SHALL have the mandatory properties and MAY have the optional properties listed in `tab-properties-share`.]

del:[Properties of a `Share` entity: Name — Definition — Data type — Cardinality and use]

del:[Properties of a `Share` entity: permission — The access granted to the recipients. The value `read` permits Read of the target. The value `update` includes `read` and permits Read and Update of the target. Delete of the target remains with the grantor. — SharePermissionCode — One (mandatory)]

del:[Properties of a `Share` entity: purpose — The purpose of the grant. This records why the recipients may access the target. The value is not empty. — CharacterString — One (mandatory)]

del:[Properties of a `Share` entity: creationTime — The time when the `Share` was created — TM Instant — One (mandatory)]

del:[Properties of a `Share` entity: endTime — The time when the grant expires. If this property is absent, the grant remains in force until the `Share` is deleted. — TM Instant — Zero or One (optional)]

del:[Properties of a `Share` entity: description — A short description of the `Share` — CharacterString — Zero or One (optional)]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/share/relations`: An implementation SHALL allow each `Share` to have direct associations to other entity types listed in `tab-relations-from-share` and `tab-relations-to-share`.]

del:[Direct relation from a `Share` entity to other entity types: Relation name — Entity type — Cardinality — Description]

del:[Direct relation from a `Share` entity to other entity types: Party — Party — One — A `Share` has one `Party`. That party is the grantor and is the `Party` of the target `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`.]

del:[Direct relation from a `Share` entity to other entity types: Recipients — Party — One to Many — A `Share` has one-to-many recipient `Parties`.]

del:[Direct relation from a `Share` entity to other entity types: PartyLocation — PartyLocation — Zero or One — A `Share` has zero-to-one `PartyLocation`. See `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/share/target`.]

del:[Direct relation from a `Share` entity to other entity types: PartyActivity — PartyActivity — Zero or One — A `Share` has zero-to-one `PartyActivity`. See `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/share/target`.]

del:[Direct relation from a SensorThings or STAplus entity type to `Share`: Relation name — Source Entity type — Cardinality — Description]

del:[Direct relation from a SensorThings or STAplus entity type to `Share`: Shares — Party — Zero to Many — A `Party` has zero-to-many `Shares` for which that party is the grantor.]

del:[Direct relation from a SensorThings or STAplus entity type to `Share`: ReceivedShares — Party — Zero to Many — A `Party` has zero-to-many `Shares` for which that party is a recipient.]

del:[Direct relation from a SensorThings or STAplus entity type to `Share`: Shares — PartyLocation — Zero to Many — A `PartyLocation` has zero-to-many `Shares`.]

del:[Direct relation from a SensorThings or STAplus entity type to `Share`: Shares — PartyActivity — Zero to Many — A `PartyActivity` has zero-to-many `Shares`.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/share/target`: A `Share` SHALL have exactly one of `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`. The count of those associations SHALL be 1. Access to a `PartyLocation` does not grant access to a `PartyActivity`, and access to a `PartyActivity` does not grant access to a `PartyLocation`.]

del:[NOTE: The STAplus 1.1 UML records this as the invariant `count(partyLocation)+count(partyActivity)==1`. A grant can be selective to one activity at one location, or to one location for an activity. Multiple `Share` entities are required to share a pair of `PartyLocation` and `PartyActivity`.]

del:[Requirements Class *Sharing*]

del:[The Sharing Requirements Class defines the access that a valid `Share` grants. A `Share` is valid when the grantor owns the target, the authenticated user is a recipient, `endTime` is absent or in the future, and `permission` covers the requested operation.]

del:[Requirements Class `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req-class/sharing` (Sharing)]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/authentication`: Read, Create, Update, and Delete access to a `Share` entity SHALL require that the user is authenticated. Read of a `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity` under a `Share` SHALL require that the user is authenticated.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/valid`: A `Share` SHALL be valid for an authenticated user and a requested operation only when all of the following hold: * the `Party` association identifies the `Party` of the target `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`; * the authenticated user identifier (e.g., `REMOTE_USER`) is identical to the `authId` of a `Party` in `Recipients`; * `endTime` is absent, or the current time is before `endTime`; * `permission` is `read` and the operation is Read, or `permission` is `update` and the operation is Read or Update; * `restricted` on the target is absent or false.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/read-share`: If the implementation supports Read of `Share`, it SHALL return a `Share` to the grantor whose `Party/authId` is identical to the authenticated user identifier, and to each recipient whose `Party/authId` is identical to the authenticated user identifier. The implementation SHALL NOT return a `Share` to any other user. This includes access via an entity URL, a collection URL, and a navigationLink.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/create`: If the implementation supports Create of `Share`, it SHALL create the entity only when the `Party` association identifies the `Party` whose `authId` is identical to the authenticated user identifier, that `Party` is the `Party` of the target `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`, `purpose` is a non-empty CharacterString, and `restricted` on the target is absent or false. The collection URL SHALL be `Shares`.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/update-share`: If the implementation supports Update of `Share`, it SHALL update the entity only when the grantor `Party/authId` is identical to the authenticated user identifier. The implementation SHALL NOT change the `Party` association to a `Party` whose `authId` is not identical to the authenticated user identifier.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/delete-share`: If the implementation supports Delete of `Share`, it SHALL delete the entity only when the grantor `Party/authId` is identical to the authenticated user identifier.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/read-target`: If a `Share` is valid for Read, the implementation SHALL permit the authenticated recipient to Read the target `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`. This includes access via an entity URL, a collection URL, and a navigationLink. Delete of the target SHALL remain restricted to the grantor as defined in the GDPR Requirements Class.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/update-target`: If a `Share` is valid for Update, the implementation SHALL permit the authenticated recipient to Update the target `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity`. The implementation SHALL NOT permit a recipient to change the `Party` association of the target.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/no-inheritance`: Access granted by a `Share` SHALL apply only to the target `PartyLocation` or `PartyActivity` of that `Share`. The implementation SHALL NOT grant access to a related entity through `$expand` or a navigationLink unless a valid `Share` exists for that related entity.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/mqtt`: If the implementation supports MQTT Subscribe for STAplus 1.1 entities, it SHALL apply the same authorization to MQTT Subscribe of `Share`, `PartyLocation`, and `PartyActivity` as this Requirements Class applies to HTTP Read.]

del:[Requirement `http://www.opengis.net/spec/sensorthings-staplus/1.1/req/sharing/business-logic`: Access that this Requirements Class permits MAY be further restricted by the implementation's Business Logic. The Business Logic SHALL NOT grant Read, Create, Update, or Delete access to a `Share`, `PartyLocation`, or `PartyActivity` beyond the restrictions of this Requirements Class and the GDPR Requirements Class.]
