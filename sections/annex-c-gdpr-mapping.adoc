== Mapping to the GDPR

This annex maps provisions of the GDPR (<<GDPR>>) to the requirements of this Profile. The mapping indicates which technical measures the GDPR conformance class provides; it is not legal advice and it does not replace the assessment by the controller.

[[tab-gdpr-mapping]]
.GDPR provisions and the requirements of this Profile
[cols="25,45,30",options="header"]
|===
|GDPR provision |Measure in this Profile |Requirements

|Art. 4(1) personal data, Recital 26 pseudonymous data
|Personal data register including indirect location and time data and the attribution chain; the roles that take data out of scope only for operator-managed identities
|<<req_gdpr_personal-data-register>>, <<req_gdpr_party-role>>

|Art. 5(1)(a) lawfulness, fairness, transparency
|Effective Agreement as legal basis for storage and publication; privacy notice
|<<req_gdpr_agreement-effective>>, <<req_gdpr_store-direct>>, <<req_gdpr_release-direct>>, <<req_gdpr_privacy-notice>>

|Art. 5(1)(b) purpose limitation
|`Agreement/purpose` and immutable Agreements; no query that indexes `License/attributionText`
|<<req_gdpr_agreement-properties>>, <<req_gdpr_agreement-immutable>>, <<req_gdpr_attribution-filter>>

|Art. 5(1)(c) data minimisation
|No storage of direct personal data without Agreement; generalization of location and time
|<<req_gdpr_store-direct>>, <<req_gdpr_release-location>>, <<req_gdpr_release-time>>

|Art. 5(1)(e) storage limitation
|Erasure on withdrawal or expiry; erasure or anonymization on deletion of the Party
|<<req_gdpr_store-direct>>, <<req_gdpr_erasure>>

|Art. 5(1)(f) integrity and confidentiality
|Access control for Agreements; release control; query consistency; no query on attribution text; TLS

|Art. 5(2) accountability; Art. 7(1) demonstrating consent
|Agreement record with `grantedTime`, `definition` and optional `proof`; retention of withdrawn records
|<<req_gdpr_agreement-create>>, <<req_gdpr_agreement-withdrawal>>, <<rec_gdpr_proof>>

|Art. 6(1)(a) consent, Art. 6(1)(b) contract
|`Agreement/legalBasis` values `consent` and `contract`
|<<req_gdpr_agreement-properties>>

|Art. 7(2) distinguishable, specific consent
|One Agreement per purpose, property set, operation set and audience

|<<req_gdpr_agreement-withdrawal>>

|Art. 12 to 14 transparent information

|Full-precision access to own data and Agreements via `me()`
|<<req_gdpr_access-right>>

|Art. 16 right to rectification

|Art. 18 right to restriction
|Partially: withdrawal of `publish` Agreements restricts release; further restriction is organisational

|Art. 20 right to data portability
|Data returned to the data subject in the structured, machine-readable SensorThings JSON encoding

|Art. 21 right to object

|Art. 25 data protection by design and by default

|Art. 32 security of processing

|Art. 35 data protection impact assessment

|Art. 5(1)(c) data minimisation, Art. 25 by design and by default (DGGS)

|Precision of publication as a parameter of the Agreement (`zoneLevel`)

|Art. 5(1)(f) confidentiality, Recital 26 anonymous information (DGGS)
|Control of Cell navigation and fine Cells; minimum number of distinct contributors and protection against differencing for aggregates

|Art. 89 safeguards for statistical purposes (DGGS)
