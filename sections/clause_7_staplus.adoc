[[dggs-staplus]]
== DGGS STAplus Requirements

=== Overview

The <<conformance_class_staplus>> Conformance Class is optional. An implementation that claims it SHALL also conform to <<OGC22-022r2>> (STAplus 1.0.1). An implementation that conforms to <<OGC26-040>> del:[(STAplus 1.1)] add:[(STAplus Party Context 1.0)] del:[uses the same `Party` model, including `role` `system`.] add:[extends STAplus 1.0.1 and therefore uses the same `Party` model, including `role` `system`.] Contributor citation on an attribution license follows the Business Logic of the STAplus standard that the implementation claims.

When this Conformance Class is claimed, each aggregate `MultiDatastream` is owned by a `Party` whose `role` is `system`. That `Party` is the producer of the aggregate. That association identifies aggregate `MultiDatastreams` among other streams.

Creator identification of the aggregate follows `{identifier-staplus}/req/business-logic/attribution` in <<OGC22-022r2>>. `Party/displayName` and `Party/authId` are not creator identification. When the `License` of the aggregate identifies an attribution license, contributors are identified by reference to the source `License` instances. Their `attributionText` is not copied onto the aggregate.

NOTE: The Requirements Class in this section is part of the Conformance Class <<conformance_class_staplus>>.

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: {identifier}/req/staplus/aggregate-party

Each aggregate `MultiDatastream` SHALL be owned by a `Party`. That `Party` SHALL have `role` equal to the CharacterString `system`. That `Party` is the producer of the aggregate.

`Party/displayName` and `Party/authId` SHALL NOT be used as creator identification of the aggregate. When the `License` of the aggregate identifies an attribution license, contributor identification SHALL follow the attribution requirement of the STAplus standard that the implementation claims. For <<OGC22-022r2>>, that requirement is `{identifier-staplus}/req/business-logic/attribution`: contributors are identified by reference to the source `License` instances, and the aggregate `License` does not receive a copy of their `attributionText`.
====
